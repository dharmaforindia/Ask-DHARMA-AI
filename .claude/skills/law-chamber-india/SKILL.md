# Running the chamber (India)

> Winning cases depends as much on the office as on the courtroom: no missed date, no lost
> document, no unhappy client, no ethical slip. This skill gives the systems and the templates.
> The ethics rules here bind every other skill in this suite.

## Non-negotiable office systems

1. **One limitation diary, checked daily.** Every order, judgment and notice gets its deadline
   entered the day it arrives, with a reminder 15 days and 3 days before. Count the deadline from the
   correct date and exclude the time allowed by law (for example s.12 Limitation Act).
2. **Every matter has a case diary**: every date, what happened, the next date, the next step and who
   is doing it.
3. **The client hears from the chamber after every hearing**, in writing, the same day or next day.
4. **Originals are logged in and out.** Never take original documents to court without a list and a
   copy set.
5. **Conflict check before any advice.** Search the client list for every party, including the
   opposite party and related companies.
6. **Fees are agreed in writing at the start.** No fee contingent on the result.

## Using AI with client data (this suite included)

- Treat client information as confidential (BSA s.132 professional privilege; BCI Rules). Share
  with Claude only what the task needs.
- Where possible, use initials or placeholders for names, phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, bank details
  and addresses, and put the real details in at the final stage.
- **Never** include the identity of a victim of a sexual offence or a child victim in a document that
  may be filed or shared (s.72 BNS; s.23 POCSO).
- Every citation and fact in AI-assisted work must be checked by the advocate before it goes to a
  client or a court. The advocate who signs is responsible for it.
- Do not paste sealed-cover material, in-camera proceedings or documents under a confidentiality
  order into any tool unless the order and the client permit it.
