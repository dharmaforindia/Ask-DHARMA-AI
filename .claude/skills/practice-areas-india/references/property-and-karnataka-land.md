## 1. Core property law

| Topic | Law | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| Sale | s.54 Transfer of Property Act (TPA) | A sale of immovable property worth Rs. 100 or more must be by registered instrument. An agreement to sell creates no interest in the property |
| Part performance | s.53A TPA | A defence for a transferee in possession who is ready and willing; the agreement must be registered since 2001 (s.17(1A) Registration Act) |
| Lis pendens | s.52 TPA | A transfer during a suit is subject to the decree |
| Leases | ss.105 to 117 TPA | Notice to quit under s.106; registration under s.107 |
| Mortgages | ss.58 onwards TPA | Equitable mortgage by deposit of title deeds in notified towns |
| Gifts | ss.122 to 129 TPA | Registered instrument and acceptance |
| Registration | Registration Act, 1908, ss.17 and 49 | Compulsorily registrable documents; effect of non-registration |
| Specific performance | Specific Relief Act, 1963 (as amended in 2018) | Specific performance is now the rule, not a discretionary relief (s.10). Readiness and willingness must be pleaded and proved (s.16(c)). Limitation 3 years (Art 54) |
| Recovery of possession | s.5 (on title, 12 years under Art 65) and s.6 (summary, within 6 months of dispossession, no title needed) Specific Relief Act | |
| Injunctions | ss.36 to 42 Specific Relief Act; Order XXXIX CPC | A permanent injunction is refused where the plaintiff's title is under a cloud; seek declaration too |
| Adverse possession | Art 65 Limitation Act | Open, continuous, hostile possession for 12 years. Can be used by a plaintiff, not only as a defence (*Ravinder Kaur Grewal v. Manjit Kaur*, (2019) 8 SCC 729) |
| GPA sales | | Do not transfer title (*Suraj Lamp & Industries*, (2012) 1 SCC 656) |
| Partition | Partition Act, 1893; CPC Order XX Rule 18 | Preliminary decree declaring shares, then a final decree. Daughters are coparceners by birth (*Vineeta Sharma*, (2020) 9 SCC 1) |

**Pitfalls:** suing for an injunction alone when title is disputed; failing to plead readiness and
willingness throughout; missing co-owners or coparceners as parties; an incorrect property schedule
(survey number, hissa or boundaries); not seeking possession along with a declaration (the proviso
to s.34 Specific Relief Act bars a bare declaration where further relief is available).

## 2. Karnataka land and revenue law

**Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964 (KLR Act):**

- **Record of rights** (RTC / pahani): entries are made under s.128 (acquisition of rights must be
  reported) and s.129 (mutation inquiry). Entries carry a presumption of correctness (s.133), but
  they do **not** create or extinguish title. Title is decided by the civil court.
- **Appeals and revisions** against revenue entries: to the Assistant Commissioner, then the Deputy
  Commissioner (s.136(2) and (3)) [VERIFY the current scheme].
- **Conversion** of agricultural land to non-agricultural use: s.95. Recent amendments have
  simplified it (deemed conversion) [VERIFY].
- The **Tahsildar** (taluk), the **Assistant Commissioner** (sub-division) and the **Deputy
  Commissioner** (district) are the main revenue authorities. The **Karnataka Appellate Tribunal**
  hears certain revenue appeals.

**Karnataka Land Reforms Act, 1961:**

- Tenancy rights vested in tenants under the 1974 amendment, by occupancy orders of the Land
  Tribunal (Form 7 applications) [VERIFY].
- **Restrictions on purchase** of agricultural land by non-agriculturists (ss.79A, 79B, 80) were
  substantially removed by the 2020 amendment [VERIFY the current position].
- s.63: ceiling limits.

**Karnataka SC and ST (Prohibition of Transfer of Certain Lands) Act, 1978 (PTCL Act):**

- A transfer of land **granted** to SC/ST persons, in breach of the grant conditions or without the
  Government's permission, is void (s.4). The Assistant Commissioner can resume and restore the land
  (s.5).
- **Delay:** an application made after an unreasonable delay fails (*Shardhamma v. Deputy
  Commissioner*, [2025] 4 SCR 2045 : 2025 INSC 583 ✓, a 1992 application for a 1969 sale). The State
  amended the Act in 2023 to address delay [VERIFY the text and how courts have read it since].
- Always check grant records (saguvali chit, darkhast file) before advising on any granted land.

## 3. Land acquisition

- Under the 2013 Act: social impact assessment, the preliminary notification (s.11), the declaration
  (s.19), the award (s.23), compensation (First Schedule), reference to the Authority (s.64), and
  lapse provisions (s.24(2)) as interpreted in *Indore Development Authority v. Manoharlal*, (2020)
  8 SCC 129 (Constitution Bench).
- BDA and KIADB acquisitions are under their own Acts, with the 2013 Act's compensation provisions
  applied where the law requires [VERIFY].
- Challenges: no public purpose, non-application of mind to objections, discrimination in
  de-notification, delay; seek enhanced compensation by reference.

## 4. Glossary of Karnataka revenue and property terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| RTC / Pahani | Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops: the annual land record showing the owner, extent, cultivation and liabilities. Available online through the Bhoomi system |
| Mutation (MR) | Mutation register entry changing the name in the record of rights after a sale, inheritance or partition |
| Survey number / Hissa | The land's survey number; hissa is a subdivision of it (e.g. Sy. No. 45/2) |
| Acre and gunta | 1 acre = 40 guntas; 1 gunta is about 1,089 sq ft |
| Akarband | Survey register showing each survey number's extent, classification and assessment |
| Tippan / Podi / Phodi | Survey sketch; podi is the subdivision of a survey number into new numbers |
| Kharab (A and B) | Uncultivable land within a survey number. "B kharab" is reserved for public purposes and cannot be privately transferred |
| Saguvali chit | Grant certificate issued for government land granted for cultivation |
| Darkhast | Application for, or proceedings on, a grant of government land |
| Hakku patra | Title deed issued by the Government (for example, for house sites) |
| Inam | Land granted free of tax (or at a reduced tax) under historic grants, abolished by statute with re-grant to holders |
| Khata | The municipal assessment register entry for urban property; the khata certificate and khata extract. Khata is not title |
| A-khata / B-khata | A-khata for properties fully compliant with building and layout rules; B-khata for those that are not (Bengaluru) |
| E-Swathu / E-Khata | Online property records for rural (panchayat) and urban properties |
| Form 11B / Form 9 / Form 11 | Panchayat property records in rural Karnataka [VERIFY current form names] |
| Encumbrance certificate (EC) | Certificate from the Sub-Registrar of registered transactions on the property |
| DC conversion | The Deputy Commissioner's order permitting non-agricultural use of agricultural land |
| Mahazar / Panchanama | Spot inspection or seizure record, signed by witnesses |
| Tahsildar / RI / VA | The taluk revenue officer / Revenue Inspector / Village Accountant (now Village Administrative Officer) |
| AC / DC | Assistant Commissioner (sub-division) / Deputy Commissioner (district) |
| Guidance value | The government-notified minimum value used for stamp duty and registration |
| OC / CC | Occupancy certificate / commencement certificate for buildings |

## 5. Strategy notes

- Revenue entries are evidence of possession and are useful, but title must be proved by documents.
  Do not rely on RTC entries alone.
- In land disputes, check for PTCL, land reforms, inam and acquisition issues before anything else.
  They can defeat a perfect-looking chain of title.
- For urgent protection of possession, combine a suit for declaration and injunction with an
  application for a temporary injunction and a court commissioner to record the spot position.
- Advise clients to pay tax, keep the khata and RTC updated, and fence and use the land: possession
  evidence wins injunction applications.
