# Notices, deeds and agreements

Stamp duty and registration decide whether a document can be relied on in court. For every deed,
check the article and rate under the **Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957** and whether registration is
compulsory under **s.17 of the Registration Act, 1908**. An unregistered document that must be
registered does not affect the property and is generally not admissible as evidence of the
transaction (s.49), apart from the limited uses in the proviso. Items marked [VERIFY] need checking
against the current Act, rates and Karnataka amendments.

---

## 1. Reply to a legal notice

```
To: [Advocate for the sender], [address]
Sub: Reply to your notice dated ___ issued on behalf of ___.

Under instructions from my client ___, I reply as follows:
1. Your notice was received on ___.
2. The allegations in the notice are denied except what is expressly admitted here.
   [Do not admit facts the client cannot later dispute. Avoid giving details that help the other
   side's case. Do not threaten criminal proceedings to recover a civil claim.]
3. The true facts are: ___ (brief, documented).
4. Your client's demand is untenable because ___.
5. [If useful: my client is willing to resolve the matter by ___ / through mediation.]
6. My client reserves the right to take appropriate proceedings, including for costs.
[Advocate's name, enrolment no., date.]
```

## 2. Notice under s.80 CPC (before suing the Government or a public officer)

Address it to the officer named in s.80(1) (for the State Government, the Secretary to the State
Government or the Collector of the district). State the name, description and residence of the
person giving notice, the cause of action and the relief claimed, in enough detail for the
Government to decide the claim. A suit can be filed only after two months from delivery, unless
the court grants leave under s.80(2) for urgent relief.

---

## 3. Notice terminating a lease (s.106 Transfer of Property Act, 1882)

In the absence of a contract or local law to the contrary: a lease for agricultural or
manufacturing purposes is terminable by six months' notice; any other lease (month to month) by
15 days' notice. The notice must be in writing, signed, and sent by post or delivered. Since the
2002 amendment, a notice is not invalid merely because the period stated falls short, if the suit
is filed after the period expires [VERIFY]. Check whether the Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 applies to
the premises before relying on s.106.

---

## 4. Agreement to sell (immovable property)

Key clauses:

1. **Parties** with full details, and for a company or firm, the authorising resolution.
2. **Schedule of the property:** survey no., hissa, extent (acres and guntas), village, hobli,
   taluk, district, boundaries; or for a site or flat: khata number, the municipal number, the
   layout approval and the plan.
3. **Title:** the seller's source of title (chain of documents), that the property is free of
   encumbrances, litigation, acquisition notifications and claims; the list of title documents
   handed over or to be handed over; the PTCL Act, land reforms and conversion status for land.
4. **Price, advance and balance**, with the mode and dates of payment.
5. **Time:** whether time is of the essence; the period for completing the sale.
6. **Seller's obligations:** obtain the encumbrance certificate, khata transfer, NOCs, conversion,
   and the consent of all co-owners and family members with a share (to avoid later partition
   claims).
7. **Possession:** when, and whether any possession is given under the agreement (this affects
   stamp duty and the protection of part performance under s.53A TPA).
8. **Default:** refund of the advance with interest or forfeiture of the advance (keep forfeiture
   reasonable), and the right to seek specific performance.
9. **Dispute resolution** and jurisdiction.
10. **Stamp duty** under the Karnataka Stamp Act (the rate rises where possession is given)
    [VERIFY the current article and rate]. Registration is compulsory where the purchaser relies on
    part performance under s.53A TPA (s.17(1A) Registration Act).

## 5. Sale deed

Recitals (the seller's title, the agreement, the consideration and its receipt), the operative
words of conveyance, the schedule, the covenants (title, quiet enjoyment, freedom from
encumbrances, indemnity, further assurances), the delivery of possession and documents, the
seller's undertaking to cooperate in mutation and khata transfer, and attestation by two witnesses.
It must be stamped on market value (guidance value) and registered. Check the Karnataka guidance
value for the area [VERIFY].

---

## 6. General Power of Attorney (GPA)

State the specific powers (manage, lease, appear before authorities, sign and present documents
for registration, sell). A power to sell immovable property should be registered and stamped
under the Stamp Act. "GPA sales" (an agreement to sell, a GPA and a will together) do not transfer
title [VERIFY: *Suraj Lamp & Industries v. State of Haryana*, (2012) 1 SCC 656]. A power of attorney
ends on the principal's death, unless the agent has an interest in the subject matter (s.202
Indian Contract Act).

---

## 7. Will

- The testator's details, a declaration of a sound and disposing mind and free will, the
  revocation of earlier wills, the appointment of an executor, a clear schedule of assets and
  bequests, a residuary clause, and provision if a beneficiary dies first.
- Signed by the testator and attested by **two witnesses** who each saw the testator sign
  (s.63 Indian Succession Act). A beneficiary should not be an attesting witness.
- Registration is optional but useful. Probate is not compulsory for wills made in Bengaluru or
  elsewhere in Karnataka (s.57 makes it compulsory only in certain areas) [VERIFY].
- To prove a will in court, at least one attesting witness must be examined if alive (s.67 BSA,
  old s.68 Evidence Act) [VERIFY], and suspicious circumstances must be explained. Advise the client
  to record a short video and a doctor's certificate where capacity may be challenged.

---

## 8. Gift deed (ss.122 and 123 Transfer of Property Act)

A gift of immovable property must be made by a registered instrument, signed by the donor and
attested by two witnesses, and accepted by the donee during the donor's lifetime. Record the
acceptance in the deed (the donee's signature). Stamp duty is lower for gifts to close family
members under the Karnataka Stamp Act [VERIFY].

---

## 9. Lease or rental agreement

Parties; premises schedule; term and renewal; rent, escalation, due date and mode; security deposit
and its refund; maintenance and repairs; permitted use; no sub-letting; lock-in; termination and
notice; handing over; stamp duty. A lease from year to year or for more than one year must be by
registered instrument (s.107 TPA; s.17(1)(d) Registration Act). Check the Karnataka Rent Act, 1999
for premises it covers [VERIFY].

---

## 10. Partnership deed (Indian Partnership Act, 1932)

Firm name, place of business, partners, capital contributions, profit and loss sharing, the
duties and powers of partners, drawings, interest on capital, bank operation, admission and
retirement, death of a partner, dissolution, accounts and dispute resolution. Register the firm
with the Registrar of Firms: an unregistered firm cannot sue third parties or its partners to
enforce a contract right (s.69).

---

## 11. MoU or term sheet

State clearly which clauses are binding (confidentiality, exclusivity, costs, governing law,
dispute resolution) and which are not (the commercial terms until the definitive agreement). Give
a long-stop date. An MoU that reads as a concluded contract will be treated as one.

---

## 12. Non-disclosure agreement (NDA)

Definition of confidential information and its exclusions (public, already known, independently
developed, legally compelled), permitted purpose, permitted recipients, standard of care, return or
destruction, term and survival, remedies (injunction), governing law and jurisdiction.

---

## 13. Family settlement or partition

- A **memorandum of family settlement** that only records an arrangement already made orally may not
  need registration. A document that itself creates or transfers rights in immovable property does
  [VERIFY: *Kale v. Deputy Director of Consolidation*, (1976) 3 SCC 119]. Draft with this distinction
  in mind.
- A **partition deed** must be stamped and registered. Include every coparcener and sharer,
  including daughters (who are coparceners by birth under the 2005 amendment) [VERIFY: *Vineeta
  Sharma v. Rakesh Sharma*, (2020) 9 SCC 1], with a schedule for each share.

---

## 14. Affidavit (general)

```
AFFIDAVIT
I, ___, aged ___, son / daughter / wife of ___, residing at ___, do solemnly affirm and state:
1. I am the ___ in the above matter and know the facts.
2. ___ (facts only, in numbered paragraphs).
VERIFICATION: I verify that the contents of paras ___ are true to my knowledge and paras ___ are
based on information received from ___, which I believe to be true. Nothing material has been
concealed. Verified at ___ on ___.
DEPONENT
[Sworn before a Notary / Oath Commissioner; identified by ___, Advocate.]
```

---

## 15. Indemnity bond

The indemnifier, the indemnified, the event or loss covered, the amount or limit, the duration and
how a claim is made. Stamp it under the Karnataka Stamp Act [VERIFY the article].

---

## 16. Authority letter / board resolution for litigation

"RESOLVED THAT Mr./Ms. ___, [designation], is authorised to institute, defend and conduct [the
proceeding] on behalf of the Company, to sign and verify pleadings, affidavits and vakalatnamas, to
engage advocates, and to do all acts necessary for it." Certified by a director or the company
secretary, with the date and place of the meeting.
