# Civil pleadings and applications

Provisions are from the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (CPC) and the Limitation Act, 1963 unless
stated otherwise. In commercial suits, the CPC applies as amended by the Commercial Courts Act, 2015
(statement of truth, stricter written-statement and denial rules). Items marked [VERIFY] need
checking against the bare Act.

---

## 1. Plaint (Order VII Rule 1)

```
IN THE COURT OF THE ___ CIVIL JUDGE (SR. DN.) / CITY CIVIL JUDGE, AT ___
O.S. NO. ___ OF 20__  [Commercial: COM.O.S. NO. ___]

PLAINTIFF: [name, age, occupation, address]
DEFENDANT(S): [name, age, occupation, address]

PLAINT UNDER ORDER VII RULE 1 READ WITH SECTION ___ OF THE CPC [AND SECTION ___ OF ___ ACT]

1. Parties: ___.
2. Facts: ___ (date order; documents by number in the list of documents).
3. Cause of action: arose on ___ when ___, and on ___ when ___. It is continuing.
4. Jurisdiction: the suit property is situated / the defendant resides / the cause of action
   arose at ___, within this Court's jurisdiction (ss.16 to 20 CPC). The suit is valued within the
   Court's pecuniary jurisdiction.
5. Limitation: the suit is within time under Article ___ of the Limitation Act because ___.
6. Valuation and court fee: the suit is valued at Rs. ___ for the purpose of jurisdiction and
   Rs. ___ for court fee under section ___ of the Karnataka Court-fees and Suits Valuation Act,
   1958; court fee of Rs. ___ is paid.
7. [Commercial suits] Pre-institution mediation under s.12A CCA was held and ended on ___
   (non-starter report enclosed) / urgent interim relief is sought, so s.12A does not apply.
8. No other suit or proceeding on the same cause of action is pending or has been decided
   [or: details of earlier proceedings].

PRAYER: The plaintiff prays for a judgment and decree:
a) declaring that ___;
b) directing the defendant to ___ / for recovery of Rs. ___ with interest at ___% from ___;
c) permanently restraining the defendant from ___;
d) costs; and
e) such other relief as the Court deems fit.

SCHEDULE: [Property: survey no., hissa, extent in acres and guntas, village, hobli, taluk,
district, boundaries on all four sides.]

VERIFICATION (O.VI r.15): Paras ___ are true to my knowledge; paras ___ are based on information
received from ___, which I believe to be true. Verified at ___ on ___.
[Commercial suits: Statement of Truth in the form in the Appendix to the CPC as amended by the CCA.]
[List of documents under O.VII r.14; copies for defendants; process fee.]
```

Notes: For a suit against the Government, plead the s.80 CPC notice or annex the order granting
leave under s.80(2). For specific performance, plead readiness and willingness (s.16(c) Specific
Relief Act) as a continuing fact, from the agreement until the hearing.

## 2. Written statement (Order VIII), set-off and counter-claim

```
WRITTEN STATEMENT FILED BY DEFENDANT NO. __ UNDER ORDER VIII RULE 1 CPC

A. PRELIMINARY OBJECTIONS
   1. The suit is not maintainable because ___ (bar under s.___, O.II r.2, res judicata, arbitration
      clause, no cause of action).
   2. The suit is barred by limitation under Article ___.
   3. The suit is bad for non-joinder of ___, a necessary party.
   4. The suit is undervalued / insufficient court fee is paid.
   5. This Court lacks territorial / pecuniary jurisdiction.

B. PARA-WISE REPLY
   Para 1: admitted / denied. [Deny specifically. An evasive denial is treated as an admission
   (O.VIII rr.3 and 5). In commercial suits, give reasons for each denial and your own version
   (O.VIII r.3A as inserted by the CCA).]

C. THE DEFENDANT'S CASE (the true facts, in date order, with documents)

D. SET-OFF (O.VIII r.6) / COUNTER-CLAIM (O.VIII r.6A)
   [Plead it like a plaint: facts, cause of action, valuation, court fee, prayer.]

PRAYER: Dismiss the suit with costs [and decree the counter-claim as prayed].
VERIFICATION. [List of documents under O.VIII r.1A.]
```

Time: 30 days from service of summons, extendable up to 90 days by the court for reasons. In
commercial suits the outer limit is 120 days and the defence is forfeited after that [VERIFY the
current position].

Replication (O.VIII r.9) is filed only with the court's leave, to answer new facts in the written
statement or a counter-claim. Do not repeat the plaint.

## 3. Interlocutory applications (IAs)

Every IA has the same frame: cause title, "APPLICATION UNDER [provision] READ WITH SECTION 151 CPC",
the grounds in numbered paragraphs, the prayer, and a supporting affidavit.

| Application | Provision | What to plead | Limit or test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amendment of pleadings | O.VI r.17 | The amendment, why it is needed, that it does not change the nature of the suit, and (after trial has begun) why it could not be raised earlier despite due diligence | Proviso to r.17. Leading guidance: *Revajeetu Builders & Developers v. Narayanaswamy & Sons*, (2009) 10 SCC 84 [VERIFY] |
| Impleadment or deletion of a party | O.I r.10(2) | Why the person is necessary (no effective decree without them) or proper (needed to settle all questions) | Necessary vs proper party test |
| Rejection of the plaint | O.VII r.11 | Which clause applies ((a) no cause of action, (b) undervalued, (c) insufficient stamp, (d) barred by law, (e) not in duplicate, (f) non-compliance with r.9), shown from the plaint itself | Only the plaint's averments and documents are considered [VERIFY: *Saleem Bhai*, (2003) 1 SCC 557] |
| Setting aside an ex parte decree | O.IX r.13 | Summons not duly served, or sufficient cause for absence; explain with dates | 30 days from the decree, or from knowledge where summons was not duly served (Art 123) |
| Restoration of a suit dismissed for default | O.IX r.9 | Sufficient cause for non-appearance | 30 days (Art 122) |
| Bringing legal representatives on record | O.XXII rr.3 and 4 | Date of death, the LRs and their relationship | 90 days from death (Art 120); setting aside abatement, 60 days after that (Art 121), with s.5 condonation if late |
| Local investigation or commission | O.XXVI r.9 | Why a spot inspection is needed (encroachment, measurement, identification) and the points for the commissioner | Not to collect evidence for a party |
| Additional evidence in appeal | O.XLI r.27 | The evidence, why it was not produced earlier despite due diligence, and why the court needs it to pronounce judgment | Narrow; give reasons |
| Disobedience of injunction | O.XXXIX r.2A | The order, its service or knowledge, the specific acts of breach with dates and proof | Civil detention or attachment |
| Condonation of delay | s.5 Limitation Act | An explanation for the whole period of delay, with dates and documents, and the applicant's good faith | Does not apply to suits. A liberal approach where there is no negligence [VERIFY: *Katiji*, (1987) 2 SCC 107] |
| Temporary injunction | O.XXXIX rr.1 and 2 | See `core-litigation.md`, section 8 | The three tests |

## 4. Summary suit (Order XXXVII)

- Lies on written contracts, bills of exchange, hundis, promissory notes and liquidated debts, in
  the courts where the Order applies.
- The plaint must state that it is filed under Order XXXVII, that no relief outside the Order is
  claimed, and the inscription required by the rules.
- The defendant must enter appearance within 10 days of service, then apply for leave to defend
  within 10 days of service of the summons for judgment, showing a substantial defence or a genuine
  triable issue [VERIFY the current periods].

## 5. Regular First Appeal (RFA) memorandum (s.96, Order XLI)

```
IN THE HIGH COURT OF KARNATAKA AT ___
R.F.A. NO. ___ OF 20__

BETWEEN: [Appellant (defendant/plaintiff in the suit)]  AND: [Respondent(s)]

MEMORANDUM OF REGULAR FIRST APPEAL UNDER SECTION 96 READ WITH ORDER XLI RULE 1 CPC AGAINST THE
JUDGMENT AND DECREE DATED ___ PASSED IN O.S. NO. ___ ON THE FILE OF THE ___, ___.

The appellant, being aggrieved, prefers this appeal on the following among other
GROUNDS
1. The judgment and decree are contrary to law, the weight of evidence and the probabilities of
   the case.
2. The trial court erred in answering Issue No. __ in the [affirmative/negative] because ___
   (evidence: PW-__, Ex.P-__).
3. The trial court failed to consider ___ (Ex.D-__).
4. The trial court misread the evidence of ___.
[Concise, numbered grounds of objection without argument or narrative (O.XLI r.1(2)).]

VALUATION AND COURT FEE: ___.
PRAYER: Set aside the judgment and decree dated ___ and [dismiss / decree] the suit with costs.
[Annex the certified copy of the judgment and decree. File an IA under s.5 Limitation Act if late,
and an IA for stay under O.XLI r.5.]
```

Limitation: 90 days to the High Court (Art 116(a)), excluding the time to obtain copies (s.12).

## 6. Regular Second Appeal (RSA) memorandum (s.100 CPC)

Same frame as the RFA, plus a separate section:

```
SUBSTANTIAL QUESTIONS OF LAW
1. Whether the courts below were right in ___, ignoring ___?
2. Whether the first appellate court, having failed to independently consider the evidence on
   Issue No. __, decided the appeal in accordance with O.XLI r.31 CPC?
3. Whether the finding on ___ is perverse, being based on no evidence / on inadmissible evidence?
```

Notes: The High Court decides only the substantial questions of law it frames at admission (s.100(4)
and (5)). A question of law is "substantial" if it is debatable, not settled, and affects the
decision [VERIFY: *Hero Vinoth v. Seshammal*, (2006) 5 SCC 545]. Concurrent findings of fact are
interfered with only for perversity or misreading of evidence.

## 7. Civil revision (s.115 CPC) and petition under Art 227

- **s.115 CPC:** only against an order of a subordinate court in a case decided, where the court
  exercised jurisdiction it did not have, failed to exercise jurisdiction it had, or acted illegally
  or with material irregularity. Since the 1999 amendment (in force from 2002), no revision lies
  where the order, if made in the party's favour, would not have finally disposed of the suit. Limitation 90 days (Art 131).
- **Art 227 petition:** for interlocutory orders of trial courts that are patently perverse or
  without jurisdiction. Plead the order, the patent error and the resulting injustice. Do not
  reargue facts.

## 8. Review petition (Order XLVII Rule 1 CPC)

```
PETITION UNDER ORDER XLVII RULE 1 READ WITH SECTION 114 CPC FOR REVIEW OF THE ORDER / JUDGMENT
DATED ___ IN ___.

GROUNDS (only these are available):
a) Discovery of new and important matter or evidence which, after due diligence, was not within
   the petitioner's knowledge or could not be produced when the order was made: ___.
b) A mistake or error apparent on the face of the record: ___ (an error that is self-evident and
   needs no long reasoning to find).
c) Any other sufficient reason (analogous to the above): ___.
PRAYER: Review and recall the order dated ___ and rehear the matter.
```

Limitation: 30 days (Art 124). Review is not an appeal in disguise. Normally it goes before the
same judge.

## 9. Execution petition (Order XXI Rule 11(2))

```
EXECUTION PETITION UNDER ORDER XXI RULE 11 CPC
(tabular form)
1. Number of the suit: ___
2. Names of the parties: ___
3. Date of the decree: ___
4. Whether any appeal was preferred: ___
5. Payment or adjustment made after the decree, if any: ___
6. Previous applications for execution, with dates and results: ___
7. Amount with interest due, or other relief granted by the decree: ___
8. Amount of costs, if any, awarded: ___
9. Against whom execution is sought: ___
10. Mode in which the assistance of the Court is required:
    a) delivery of the property specifically decreed;
    b) attachment and sale of property (schedule attached);
    c) arrest and detention in civil prison;
    d) appointment of a receiver; or
    e) otherwise, as the nature of the relief requires.
VERIFICATION.
```

Limitation for executing a decree: 12 years from when it becomes enforceable (Art 136); for a
mandatory injunction, 3 years (Art 135) [VERIFY].

## 10. Compromise petition (Order XXIII Rule 3) and withdrawal (Order XXIII Rule 1)

```
JOINT MEMO / COMPROMISE PETITION UNDER ORDER XXIII RULE 3 CPC

The plaintiff and the defendant have settled the suit on the following terms:
1. ___ (precise, enforceable terms: amounts, dates, mode of payment, delivery of possession,
   withdrawal of other cases, consequences of default).
2. The parties pray that the suit be decreed in terms of this compromise.
3. [Where the compromise covers property outside the suit, list it and note registration and stamp
   requirements.]
Signed by the parties and their advocates. [Parties identified before the court.]
```

Withdrawal: under O.XXIII r.1(3), ask for permission to withdraw **with liberty to file a fresh suit**
on the same subject matter, giving the formal defect or other sufficient ground. Withdrawal without
liberty bars a fresh suit on the same cause.

## 11. Transfer petition (s.24 CPC)

Filed in the High Court or District Court. Plead the pending case, the reasons (convenience of the
parties and witnesses, a related case pending elsewhere, a reasonable apprehension of bias with
specific facts) and the court to which transfer is sought. For inter-state transfer, file in the
Supreme Court under s.25 CPC (see `constitutional-and-appellate.md`).
