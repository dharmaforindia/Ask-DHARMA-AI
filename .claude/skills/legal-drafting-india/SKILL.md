# Legal drafting (India)

> Drafts are for an enrolled advocate to review, settle, sign and file. Every Registry has its own
> current format, page setup and checklist: follow it exactly. Every citation in a draft must be
> verified or marked [VERIFY], as the `senior-advocate` skill's five rules require.

## Before you draft: get these facts

If any of these is missing, ask for it or leave a clearly marked blank `___` and list it in the
cover note. Do not invent facts, dates, amounts or document numbers.

- Court or forum, bench (Bengaluru, Dharwad or Kalaburagi), and case type.
- Full names, ages, occupations and addresses of all parties. How the State, a company, a minor or
  a deceased party is represented.
- The impugned order, decision or event: date, authority, reference number.
- Chronology with documents. Which documents are in Kannada (translations needed).
- Earlier and pending proceedings on the same subject.
- Limitation: the date time started running, and any delay to explain.
- The relief the client needs, including interim relief.
- Valuation and court fee (civil matters).

## The house style

1. **Order.** Cause title, then the nature of the proceeding and the provision, then facts, then
   grounds, then interim relief, then prayer, then verification or affidavit. Facts never go in
   the grounds, and argument never goes in the facts.
2. **Numbered paragraphs, one idea each.** Short sentences. Active voice. Plain English. Use legal
   terms where they carry meaning, and avoid empty phrases ("it is humbly submitted that" once is
   enough).
3. **Facts in date order**, each tied to its document: "By letter dated 3 March 2025 (Annexure-C,
   page 41), the respondent ...".
4. **Grounds: one point per ground, best ground first.** Each ground states the error, the law (the
   provision and the authority with its paragraph) and how it applies to these facts.
5. **Prayer.** Exact, enforceable relief. Add an alternative prayer, an interim prayer and a general
   prayer ("such other orders as this Hon'ble Court deems fit"). The prayer must match the grounds.
6. **Jurisdiction, limitation and valuation paragraphs** in every original proceeding (Order VII
   Rule 1 CPC for plaints).
7. **Disclosure.** State earlier proceedings and adverse orders. State that no material fact has
   been withheld. Suppression can defeat the whole case.
8. **Verification and affidavits.** In verification (Order VI Rule 15 CPC), separate the paragraphs
   true to the deponent's own knowledge from those based on information believed to be true, and
   name the source. Affidavits (Order XIX) must disclose the source of information. A false
   verification is an offence, so never draft a verification the client cannot honestly swear to.
9. **Annexure marking.** Supreme Court: Annexure P-1, P-2 (petitioner), R-1 (respondent). Karnataka
   High Court: Annexure-A, B, C. Trial courts: a list of documents (Order VII Rule 14 for the
   plaintiff, Order VIII Rule 1A for the defendant). File true copies and certified translations.
10. **Parties.** The State is sued through the proper officer (s.79 CPC; Art 300). A minor sues or
    is sued through a next friend or guardian (Order XXXII). A company acts through an authorised
    signatory, so annex the board resolution or authority letter.
11. **Victims' identity.** Never name or identify the victim of a sexual offence or a child victim in
    a document that may become public (s.72 BNS; s.23 POCSO Act). Use initials or "the victim" and
    file identifying documents in a sealed cover.
12. **Criminal-law sections.** New law first, old law in brackets: "s.318(4) BNS (old s.420 IPC)".
    Use the old law where the offence was committed before 1 July 2024.
13. **Proofread for consistency.** Names, dates, amounts, survey numbers and annexure numbers must
    match everywhere. Check page numbering and the index, and check that every annexure referred to
    is attached.

## Self-review before you hand over a draft

- [ ] The right forum, provision and case type. Limitation is stated, and any delay is explained.
- [ ] Every fact has a date and a document. No invented facts.
- [ ] Grounds are separate from facts, one point each, the best first.
- [ ] The prayer is exact, matches the grounds, and has alternative, interim and general prayers.
- [ ] Earlier proceedings are disclosed.
- [ ] The verification or affidavit is one the client can honestly swear to.
- [ ] Names, dates, amounts, survey numbers and annexure numbers are consistent throughout.
- [ ] No victim's identity is disclosed.
- [ ] Every citation is verified or marked [VERIFY].
- [ ] A cover note (`references/core-litigation.md`, section 11) lists assumptions, blanks to fill,
      documents to annex, the limitation date and the [VERIFY] items.
