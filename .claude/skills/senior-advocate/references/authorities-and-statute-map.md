or the India Code text in the Legal Data Hunter database on 19 November 2026 (case name, date and the

## 1. Old law to new law: main sections

BNS, BNSS and BSA came into force on 1 July 2024. The date of the offence decides whether the IPC or
the BNS applies. Proceedings pending on that date continue under the CrPC (s.531(2)(a) BNSS ✓).
