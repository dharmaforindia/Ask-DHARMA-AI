# Drafting templates

These are skeletons, not final formats. Each court's Registry has its own formats, page sizes,
margins, fonts and checklists. Follow them exactly. Fill every `___`, delete headings that do not
apply, and annex every document you refer to.

General drafting rules:

- **Facts first, law second, prayer last.** Write in numbered paragraphs, one idea per paragraph.
- Use short sentences. Use active voice where you can ("The respondent cancelled the licence on
  ___", not "The licence came to be cancelled").
- Refer to every document by annexure number and page.
- Disclose earlier proceedings and adverse orders. State that nothing material has been withheld.
- Ask for exact relief that can be enforced. Add an alternative prayer and a general prayer.
- Give new criminal-law sections first, with the old ones in brackets.
- End with a [VERIFY] list for the advocate's review (internal, not for filing).

```

[Petitioner] ... Petitioner
Versus
[Respondent(s)] ... Respondent(s)

PETITION FOR SPECIAL LEAVE TO APPEAL AGAINST THE FINAL JUDGMENT AND ORDER
DATED ___ PASSED BY THE HIGH COURT OF KARNATAKA AT ___ IN ___ NO. ___ OF ___

TO
THE HON'BLE THE CHIEF JUSTICE OF INDIA
AND HIS / HER COMPANION JUSTICES OF THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA

The humble petition of the Petitioner above-named

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:

1. The Petitioner seeks special leave to appeal against the final judgment and order dated ___
   (Annexure P-__) by which the High Court ___.

2. QUESTIONS OF LAW
   (i) Whether ___?
   (ii) Whether ___?

3. DECLARATION IN TERMS OF [the applicable Rule]
   The Petitioner states that no other petition seeking leave to appeal has been filed against the
   impugned judgment and order.

4. DECLARATION REGARDING ANNEXURES
   The Annexures P-1 to P-__ produced with this petition are true copies of documents that formed
   part of the record of the case in the court(s) below.

5. GROUNDS
   A. Because ___ [the best point first, with authority and paragraph].
   B. Because ___.
   C. Because the High Court failed to consider ___ (Annexure P-__, page __).

6. GROUNDS FOR INTERIM RELIEF
   (a) prima facie case; (b) balance of convenience; (c) irreparable harm: ___.

7. MAIN PRAYER
   (a) grant special leave to appeal against the final judgment and order dated ___;
   (b) pass such other orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit.

8. PRAYER FOR INTERIM RELIEF
   (a) stay the operation of the impugned judgment and order dated ___ pending the petition;
   (b) in the alternative, direct the parties to maintain status quo regarding ___.

AND FOR THIS ACT OF KINDNESS THE PETITIONER SHALL, AS IN DUTY BOUND, EVER PRAY.

Filed by: [Advocate-on-Record], Code No. ___
Place: New Delhi   Date: ___
```

For a criminal SLP, use "CRIMINAL APPELLATE JURISDICTION" and "SPECIAL LEAVE PETITION (CRIMINAL)",
and add proof of surrender or an application for exemption from surrendering where the petitioner
has been sentenced to imprisonment.

```
BETWEEN: [Petitioner / accused no. __] ... PETITIONER
AND: State of Karnataka, by ___ Police Station, represented by the State Public Prosecutor,
     High Court Buildings, Bengaluru. [and the complainant, if impleaded] ... RESPONDENT(S)

PETITION UNDER SECTION [483 / 482] OF THE BHARATIYA NAGARIK SURAKSHA SANHITA, 2023 PRAYING TO
[ENLARGE THE PETITIONER ON BAIL / DIRECT THAT IN THE EVENT OF ARREST THE PETITIONER BE RELEASED ON
BAIL] IN CRIME NO. ___ OF ___ POLICE STATION, FOR OFFENCES PUNISHABLE UNDER SECTIONS ___ OF THE
BHARATIYA NYAYA SANHITA, 2023, PENDING ON THE FILE OF ___.

1. Case of the prosecution in brief: ___.
2. Earlier bail application: the petitioner's application before the ___ Court was rejected on ___
   (Annexure-__) [or: reasons for approaching this Court directly].
3. GROUNDS
   a) The petitioner is innocent and has been falsely implicated. The allegations, taken as they
      stand, do not make out the offence under s.___ because ___.
   b) The offences are punishable with ___. [For up to 7 years: s.35(3) BNSS notice and the
      Arnesh Kumar guidelines apply.]
   c) Investigation is complete / the charge-sheet has been filed on ___. Custody is not needed.
   d) The petitioner has been in custody since ___ (__ days).
   e) No criminal antecedents. Permanent resident of ___ with deep roots in society. No risk of
      absconding.
   f) The petitioner will abide by any conditions, will not tamper with evidence or influence
      witnesses, and will appear as and when directed.
   g) Parity: co-accused ___ has been granted bail on ___ (Annexure-__) and the petitioner's role is
      similar or lesser.
4. PRAYER: ___.

The petitioner undertakes to abide by all conditions imposed by this Hon'ble Court.
```

## 5. Quashing petition (s.528 BNSS, old s.482 CrPC)

```
PETITION UNDER SECTION 528 OF THE BHARATIYA NAGARIK SURAKSHA SANHITA, 2023 PRAYING TO QUASH THE
[FIR / COMPLAINT / CHARGE-SHEET / ORDER TAKING COGNIZANCE] IN ___ PENDING ON THE FILE OF ___.

GROUNDS
a) Even if the allegations are taken at face value and accepted in their entirety, they do not make
   out any offence (the first category in Bhajan Lal). The ingredients of s.___ BNS are ___, and
   none is alleged.
b) The dispute is purely civil / commercial / contractual and has been given a criminal colour to
   pressure the petitioner.
c) The proceeding is manifestly attended with mala fides, filed with an ulterior motive after ___.
d) There is an express legal bar to the proceeding: ___ (e.g. absence of sanction under s.218 BNSS,
   limitation under s.514 BNSS).
e) [Compromise] The parties have settled (Annexure-__). The dispute is private in nature, and
   continuing it would be an abuse of process (Gian Singh; B.S. Joshi for matrimonial disputes).
f) Allowing the proceeding to continue is an abuse of the process of the court.

PRAYER: Quash the proceedings in ___ insofar as the petitioner is concerned.
INTERIM: Stay further proceedings [and: direct that no coercive steps be taken, giving specific
reasons, bearing in mind Neeharika Infrastructure].
```

## 10. Statement of objections / counter-affidavit (for the respondent)

```
1. Preliminary objections: maintainability, alternative remedy, delay and laches, suppression of
   facts, disputed questions of fact unsuitable for a writ, locus, non-joinder of parties.
2. Para-wise reply: "The averments in para __ are denied except what is expressly admitted.
   It is false that ___. The true facts are ___ (Annexure R-__)."
3. Additional facts the petitioner has suppressed.
4. Justification of the impugned action on its own stated reasons. An order must be judged by the
   reasons in it and cannot be supplemented by fresh reasons in an affidavit (Mohinder Singh Gill v.
   Chief Election Commissioner, (1978) 1 SCC 405 [VERIFY]). So check the order's own reasons first.
5. Prayer: dismiss the petition with costs; vacate the interim order.
```
