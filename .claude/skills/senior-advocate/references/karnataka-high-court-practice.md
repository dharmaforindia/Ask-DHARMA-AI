## Rules and instruments to know

- **Karnataka High Court Act, 1961, s.4.** An appeal lies to a Division Bench from a judgment or
  order of a single judge in the exercise of original jurisdiction, which includes Art 226. Whether a
  writ appeal lies against an order made under Art 227 (supervisory jurisdiction) depends on the
  nature of the jurisdiction actually exercised. Check the latest Full Bench and Supreme Court
  position before filing. Limitation is 30 days (Limitation Act, Art 117).
- **Writ Proceedings Rules, 1977** (High Court of Karnataka) [VERIFY].
- **High Court of Karnataka Rules, 1959** [VERIFY].
- **Karnataka Civil Rules of Practice, 1967** and **Karnataka Criminal Rules of Practice, 1968**, for
  the subordinate courts [VERIFY].
- **Karnataka Court-fees and Suits Valuation Act, 1958**.

## Filing mechanics

- Index, synopsis or list of dates, memorandum of petition or appeal, verifying affidavit, annexures
  (marked Annexure-A, B, C ...), vakalath, court fee, process fee and the addresses of respondents.
- **Translations.** Trial-court and revenue records are often in Kannada. File typed English
  translations certified as true.
- **Advance copy to government counsel.** In matters against the State, serve an advance copy on the
  office of the Government Advocate (civil and writ) or the High Court Government Pleader or State
  Public Prosecutor (criminal), and keep the acknowledgment.
- **Office objections.** Cure them within the time given. Uncured matters are listed for dismissal.
- **Caveat.** Under s.148A CPC; it lasts 90 days.

## Government counsel and statutory bodies

- State: the Advocate General, Additional Advocates General, Government Advocates (civil and writ),
  the High Court Government Pleader and the State Public Prosecutor (criminal).
- Union of India: the Additional Solicitor General, Deputy Solicitor General and Central Government
  Counsel.
- Bodies with their own panel counsel include the city corporation in Bengaluru, BDA, KIADB, KPTCL
  and the electricity supply companies, the Karnataka Housing Board, universities and others. Serve
  their panel counsel.

## Writ practice (Art 226 and Art 227)

- **Representation first.** The bench often asks whether the petitioner gave the authority a
  representation. Where it fits, give one and wait a reasonable time. A common fall-back relief is a
  direction to "consider and dispose of the representation dated ___ within ___ weeks".
- **Statement of objections** is the usual name for the State's reply. File a rejoinder only to deal
  with new facts.
- **Areas that often come up in Karnataka:**
  - Revenue records and mutation (Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964, ss.127 to 136), and conversion
    of land use (s.95) [VERIFY the sections].
  - Land acquisition by BDA and KIADB, and under the Right to Fair Compensation Act, 2013.
  - Restoration of granted lands under the Karnataka Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes
    (Prohibition of Transfer of Certain Lands) Act, 1978 (the PTCL Act). An application made after
    unreasonable delay fails (*Shardhamma v. Deputy Commissioner*, [2025] 4 SCR 2045 : 2025 INSC 583,
    29 April 2025). Check the effect of the State's 2023 amendment to the PTCL Act on delay before
    relying on this line of cases [VERIFY].
  - Karnataka Land Reforms Act, 1961, including the effect of the 2020 amendments [VERIFY].
  - Service matters: State government employees must normally go to the Karnataka State
    Administrative Tribunal first, and then to the High Court.
  - Tenders and contracts, education and admissions, municipal matters in Bengaluru and other
    cities, co-operative societies (Karnataka Co-operative Societies Act, 1959), excise, mining and
    transport permits.
  - Preventive detention: habeas corpus (WPHC).

## Criminal practice

- **Bail.** File a Criminal Petition under s.483 BNSS (regular) or s.482 BNSS (anticipatory). The
  Sessions Court and the High Court have concurrent jurisdiction, but the High Court generally
  expects the Sessions Court to be approached first, or a good reason for coming directly.
  The bail categories and guidance in *Satender Kumar Antil v. CBI*, [2022] 10 SCR 351, are often
  cited.
- **Quashing.** File a Criminal Petition under s.528 BNSS (old s.482 CrPC). Rely on the categories in
  *State of Haryana v. Bhajan Lal*, 1992 Supp (1) SCC 335 [VERIFY]. For quashing on compromise, cite
  *Gian Singh v. State of Punjab*, (2012) 10 SCC 303 [VERIFY], and for matrimonial disputes
  *B.S. Joshi v. State of Haryana* (decided 13 March 2003; (2003) 4 SCC 675 [VERIFY the SCC
  citation]).
- **Section 138 NI Act** cases are common. The presumption under s.139 covers the existence of a
  legally enforceable debt, and the accused can rebut it on a preponderance of probabilities
  (*Rangappa v. Sri Mohan*, [2010] 6 SCR 507, three judges; an appeal from Karnataka).
- **Revision** (CRL.RP), **appeal** (CRL.A) and **suspension of sentence** (s.430 BNSS).

## Civil practice

- **RFA:** a first appeal is a rehearing on facts and law. The first appellate court must deal with
  every issue and the evidence (*Santosh Hazari v. Purushottam Tiwari*, (2001) 3 SCC 179 [VERIFY]).
- **RSA:** only on a substantial question of law, framed at admission (s.100 CPC).
- **CRP under s.115 CPC** is narrow after the 1999 amendment. Supervisory jurisdiction under Art 227
  is often used instead for orders of the trial court.
- **Commercial cases:** Bengaluru has designated Commercial Courts. Appeals go to the Commercial
  Appellate Division under s.13 CCA within 60 days. Pre-institution mediation under s.12A is
  mandatory unless urgent interim relief is sought (*Patil Automation*, [2022] 11 SCR 808).

## Courtroom habits in the Karnataka High Court

- Benches often dictate orders in open court. Have the exact relief and its wording ready.
- "Short point" matters may be heard and decided in a few minutes. Prepare the two-minute version of
  every matter.
- Proceedings and pleadings are in English. Subordinate-court records are often in Kannada, so have
  the translations ready and know the key passages.
- Proceedings may be live-streamed. Conduct yourself accordingly at all times.
