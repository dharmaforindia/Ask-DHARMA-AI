# Opinions, memos and analysis formats

## 1. Research memo

```
RESEARCH MEMO
To: ___   From: ___   Date: ___   Matter: ___

1. QUESTION: ___ (one sentence)
2. SHORT ANSWER: ___ (two to four sentences, with the level of certainty: settled / the better
   view / arguable / unsettled)
3. RELEVANT FACTS: ___
4. LAW
   4.1 Statute: s.___ of ___ (text of the operative words).
   4.2 Binding authority: ___ (citation, bench, date), para __: "[quote]".
   4.3 Later treatment: ___.
   4.4 Contrary authority and how to deal with it: ___.
5. APPLICATION TO OUR FACTS: ___
6. RISKS AND OPEN QUESTIONS: ___
7. RECOMMENDATION: ___
8. VERIFICATION TABLE
   | Authority | Citation | Para | Status |
```

## 2. Legal opinion

```
LEGAL OPINION
[Letterhead]   Ref: ___   Date: ___
To: [client]   Sub: Opinion on ___

1. QUERY: You have asked whether ___.
2. DOCUMENTS EXAMINED: (list, with dates). This opinion is based only on these documents and the
   facts stated to us.
3. FACTS AS UNDERSTOOD: ___
4. ISSUES: ___
5. OPINION IN BRIEF: ___
6. ANALYSIS (issue by issue: the law, its application, the counter-view, the conclusion)
7. CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS: ___ (what to do, by when)
8. ASSUMPTIONS AND QUALIFICATIONS: the documents are genuine and complete; signatures are genuine;
   facts not disclosed may change this opinion; it is based on the law as on today; it is for the
   client's use only.
[Advocate's name, enrolment no., signature]
```

Write the opinion for the client to act on: lead with the answer, use plain words, and say what
to do. Never overstate certainty.

## 3. Title opinion (immovable property in Karnataka)

**Documents to call for:**

- Title deeds for the last **30 years** at least (sale deeds, partition deeds, gift deeds, wills
  with probate if any, court decrees, grants), and the mother deed.
- **Encumbrance certificates** for the same 30 years (Form 15 where there are entries, Form 16 where
  there are none) [VERIFY the form numbers].
- **Revenue records** for agricultural land: RTC (pahani) for the last 15 to 30 years, mutation
  register extracts, the akarband, the survey sketch (tippan / podi), the grant certificate
  (saguvali chit) and conditions if it is granted land, the land reforms tribunal order if any.
- **For urban property:** the khata certificate and khata extract, tax-paid receipts, the DC
  conversion order (agricultural to non-agricultural use), layout or building plan approvals, the
  occupancy certificate, and BDA, BMRDA or planning authority approvals [VERIFY current authority
  names for Bengaluru].
- **Restrictions:** PTCL Act status (whether the land was granted to SC/ST persons), Land Reforms Act
  status, inam lands, acquisition notifications, forest or government land claims, tank bed or
  "kharab" land.
- **Litigation search:** pending suits, lis pendens, attachments, and the persons in possession.
- **Identity:** the identity and capacity of the sellers; all family members with a share (sons
  and daughters); minors' shares (need court permission to sell); powers of attorney.

```
TITLE OPINION
Property: [full schedule]
Sellers: ___   Purchaser: ___
1. DOCUMENTS EXAMINED (list with dates and registration numbers)
2. FLOW OF TITLE (table: date | document | from | to | extent | registration no. | remarks)
3. REVENUE AND MUNICIPAL RECORDS (consistency of name, extent and survey numbers)
4. ENCUMBRANCES (from the ECs; mortgages; charges; attachments)
5. RESTRICTIONS AND APPROVALS (PTCL, land reforms, conversion, plan approvals, acquisition)
6. LITIGATION
7. DEFECTS FOUND AND HOW TO CURE THEM (release deeds, rectification, consents, court permission)
8. OPINION: The sellers have a clear and marketable title, subject to ___ / Title is not clear because ___.
9. DOCUMENTS TO BE OBTAINED BEFORE THE SALE
10. QUALIFICATIONS
```

## 4. Case brief (judgment analysis)

```
CASE BRIEF
Name and citation: ___ (date; bench and strength; author)
Court and proceeding: ___
1. FACTS (material facts only)
2. ISSUES
3. ARGUMENTS (both sides, briefly)
4. HOLDING (the answer to each issue)
5. RATIO DECIDENDI (the principle necessary for the decision), with paragraphs
6. OBITER (notable observations), with paragraphs
7. DISPOSITION (the order made)
8. LATER TREATMENT (followed / explained / distinguished / referred / overruled)
9. HOW TO USE IT: for our client, the useful paragraphs are ___
   HOW THE OTHER SIDE WILL USE IT: ___   HOW TO DISTINGUISH IT: ___
```

## 5. FIR, complaint or charge-sheet analysis

**Step 1. Ingredient table.** For every offence alleged:

| Offence (new / old section) | Ingredient | What the FIR / charge-sheet says (page, witness) | Gap or weakness |
|---|---|---|---|

**Step 2. Checklist.**

- Delay in lodging the FIR, and whether it is explained.
- Improvements and embellishments between the FIR, the s.180 BNSS (old s.161 CrPC) statements, the
  s.183 BNSS (old s.164 CrPC) statement and the evidence.
- Whether the witnesses are interested, related or independent. Whether the mahazar and panch
  witnesses are independent and whether they support the prosecution.
- Recovery: was the information recorded, who was present, was the place accessible to others.
- Forensic and medical evidence: FSL reports, the post-mortem report, the wound certificate,
  whether they match the oral account, and the chain of custody of samples.
- Electronic evidence: s.63 BSA (old s.65B) certificate, call records, CCTV, hash values.
- Procedure: arrest memo, grounds of arrest given in writing, s.35(3) BNSS notice, search and seizure
  rules, videography of search and seizure under the BNSS where required [VERIFY], sanction for
  prosecution, jurisdiction, limitation.
- Special statute safeguards (NDPS s.42, s.50, s.52A; POCSO age proof; PMLA s.19 grounds of arrest;
  SC/ST Act requirements) [see `practice-areas-india`].
- The defence version and the documents that support it.

**Step 3. Output.** For the defence: grounds for anticipatory bail, bail, quashing or discharge, and
a cross-examination map. For the complainant: the gaps the investigation must fill, and grounds
for a protest petition or further investigation.

## 6. Grounds-of-appeal analysis

For each finding in the impugned judgment:

| Para | Finding | Evidence relied on | Evidence ignored or misread | Error of law | Ground |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then classify each ground: error of law, error of jurisdiction, perversity (no evidence, or
ignoring material evidence), procedural irregularity causing prejudice, or a wrong legal test. Rank
the grounds by strength. Check the appellate court's standard of interference (for example, the
High Court in a second appeal decides only substantial questions of law; the Supreme Court under
Art 136 rarely interferes with concurrent findings of fact).

## 7. Contract or document review

```
REVIEW NOTE: [document], [date]
1. SUMMARY: what the document does, the parties, the term, the money.
2. KEY RISKS (ranked): clause | risk | suggested change
3. CHECKLIST
   - Parties and capacity; authority to sign (resolutions, powers of attorney).
   - Consideration, payment terms, interest on delay, taxes (GST).
   - Term, renewal, termination (for cause, for convenience), consequences of termination.
   - Liability: indemnities, limitation of liability, liquidated damages (s.74 Contract Act).
   - Intellectual property, confidentiality, data protection.
   - Force majeure.
   - Dispute resolution: the arbitration clause (seat, venue, number of arbitrators, institution,
     rules), governing law, exclusive jurisdiction.
   - Stamp duty (Karnataka Stamp Act) and registration requirements.
   - Notices: addresses and modes.
4. MISSING CLAUSES
5. RECOMMENDATION: sign / sign with changes / do not sign.
```

For a **notice received**: the deadline to reply or act, the claims made, the admissions to avoid,
the documents to collect, and whether a caveat should be lodged.

## 8. Compilation of authorities

```
COMPILATION OF JUDGMENTS ON BEHALF OF THE [PETITIONER]
INDEX
| Sl. | Case name and citation | Proposition | Relevant paras | Pages |
```

Put the most important authority first. Flag or highlight the relevant paragraphs. Use the
reported version (SCC, SCR or the neutral citation) and give the bench strength. Give copies to the
bench and the other side.
